# 📱 Mobile Phone Sales Performance & Business Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI analytics project designed to help businesses understand mobile phone sales performance, identify top-performing brands and models, analyze sales trends, and make data-driven decisions about product performance and revenue growth. 📊📈

## 📌 Problem Statement & Project Overview

In the competitive mobile phone market, analyzing total sales alone does not reveal which brands perform best, which models attract customers, or how sales patterns change over time.

This project transforms mobile phone sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, key performance indicators (KPIs), and data-driven analysis. The dashboard helps explore sales performance, product demand, and revenue trends to support better business decisions.

The project focuses on three major analytical areas:

1. **Sales Performance Dashboard (Revenue & Sales Overview):** Analyzes overall sales performance, revenue contribution, sales quantity, and key business metrics to evaluate overall performance.

2. **Brand & Model Analysis (Product Performance):** Compares mobile phone brands and models to identify top-selling products, understand product demand, and evaluate brand-wise performance.

3. **Sales Trends & Insights (Time-Based Analysis):** Examines sales trends across available time periods to identify growth patterns, changes in demand, and potential business opportunities.

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights & Key Metrics

### 📈 Page 1: Sales Performance & Revenue Overview

- **Total Sales Revenue:** Measures the overall revenue generated from mobile phone sales.
- **Total Units Sold:** Tracks the number of mobile phones sold during the selected period.
- **Total Transactions:** Evaluates the total number of recorded sales transactions.
- **Average Selling Price:** Analyzes the average selling price of mobile phones.
- **Revenue Trends:** Visualizes changes in sales revenue across available time periods.
- **Interactive Filters:** Enables users to explore sales performance by available brands, products, and dates.

### 📱 Page 2: Brand & Model Performance

- **Brand-Wise Sales:** Compares sales performance across different mobile phone brands.
- **Model-Wise Analysis:** Identifies popular models based on sales volume and revenue.
- **Product Contribution:** Evaluates the contribution of individual products to total sales.
- **Top-Performing Products:** Highlights leading brands and models using relevant sales metrics.
- **Comparative Analysis:** Helps identify differences in product demand and revenue contribution.

### 📊 Page 3: Sales Trends & Business Insights

- **Time-Based Sales Analysis:** Examines changes in sales performance over the available time period.
- **Sales Volume Trends:** Tracks the movement of units sold.
- **Revenue Comparison:** Compares revenue across available product categories, brands, or periods.
- **Demand Patterns:** Identifies trends in product sales to support business planning.
- **Interactive Exploration:** Uses visualizations and filters to investigate specific sales patterns.

*Note: Update the page descriptions and KPI names to match the actual pages and measures in your Power BI file.*

## 💡 Business Impact & Key Insights

### 📈 Revenue & Sales Performance Optimization

Analyzing revenue and unit sales helps businesses understand overall performance, compare sales periods, and identify opportunities to improve revenue generation.

### 🎯 Product & Brand Strategy

Comparing brand-wise and model-wise performance helps businesses recognize popular products, evaluate product demand, and make more informed product selection and promotional decisions.

### 📦 Inventory & Demand Planning

Sales volume and time-based trends can help businesses identify products with stronger demand and support inventory planning, subject to stock availability and replenishment data.

### 📊 Data-Driven Decision-Making

Interactive reports enable users to explore detailed sales information, compare performance indicators, and communicate findings through clear visualizations.
